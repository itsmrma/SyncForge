### Short / Punchy

Free & open source on GitHub.

### LinkedIn / Technical
Media remuxing shouldn't require juggling cryptic command-line flags or lossy video re-encodings.

SyncForge is a high-performance native desktop application written in Rust with Tauri v2 and React 19. It batch-processes video, audio, and subtitle streams with:
• WaveSync: Pure-Rust FFT cross-correlation delay calculation (`hound` + `rustfft`)
• Smart Episode Auto-Matching: Instant season batch pairing
• Track Inspector: 1-click filtering (`No Commentary/SDH`, language presets)
• Lossless MKVToolNix remuxing & optional libopus audio encoding

Free, open-source, and available on Windows, Linux, and macOS.
